Live captions

Google Chrome can write captions for a lecture as it plays, in Thai and in English. It takes about two minutes to set up, and only once.

Computers only. Live captions work in Google Chrome on a Windows or Mac computer. They do not work on an iPad, iPhone or Android phone, or in Safari, Edge or Firefox.

  2. Open it in a new tab

    Press {{ keys.newTab }} for a new tab, paste with {{ keys.paste }}, then press {{ keys.go }}.

    Or click Chrome's ⋮ menu (top right), then Settings, then Accessibility.

  3. Turn on Live Caption

    Click the switch next to Live Caption so it turns blue.

    Shortcut: the captions button in Flick's player, next to full screen, moves the lecture into a small window. Click the captions button at its bottom right and turn on Live Caption there.

  4. Add both Thai and English

    Under Supported Live Caption languages, click Add languages.

    Type Thai in the search box, tick Thai - ไทย, then click Add.

    English is usually in the list already. If it is not, add it the same way. Lectures are mostly in Thai with English terms, and some are given in English, so you need both.

  5. Make Thai the default

    Click ⋮ next to Thai, then Make default.

    Wait until both languages say Speech files downloaded, with Thai marked (default).

    For a lecture given in English, make English the default the same way.

  6. Play the lecture

    Go back to the lecture's tab and press play. Captions appear in a box at the bottom of the screen, which you can drag anywhere.

If something does not work

  • No captions? Check that both languages have finished downloading, and that the lecture is playing.
  • Captions may fall behind at high speed. They keep up best at 1.5× or slower.
  • To turn captions off, turn off the same Live Caption switch.
