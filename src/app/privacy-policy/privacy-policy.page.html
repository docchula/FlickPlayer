MDCU Recorded Lecture ("the app") is operated by the Student Union of the Faculty of Medicine, Chulalongkorn University for students and staff of the Faculty. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Account information

Signing in requires a Google account on the docchula.com domain. Firebase Authentication receives your name, email address, profile photo, and account ID from Google. This is used to verify you're a member of the Faculty and to identify your own watch history and course access — it is not shared with anyone outside the systems described below.

Lecture watch history

To let you resume lectures where you left off and to support course evaluation, the app records which videos you watch, how much of each you completed, and when, tied to your account. This tracking is core to the app's functionality and is not affected by the analytics consent choice below.

Analytics (optional)

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how features are used and to improve the app. This is only active if you accept the analytics banner shown on your first visit. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this app, which will show the banner again.

When enabled, analytics collects:

  • Pages/screens you visit within the app.
  • Search terms you enter.
  • Use of the Pomodoro study timer and custom video playback speed setting.
  • Your account ID.

Analytics data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

App performance monitoring

The app uses Firebase Performance Monitoring and Firebase Remote Config to measure technical performance (e.g. load times) and to manage app configuration. These collect technical data about your device and network, not your personal identity, and run independently of the analytics consent choice.

Data retention

Watch history is retained for as long as your account is active, to support resuming lectures and course evaluation. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics' standard retention settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be directed to the Student Union of the Faculty of Medicine, Chulalongkorn University.
